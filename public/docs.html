Rebound

Rebound is the tab in Bouncer where you make and chat with bots. A bot is a named persona with its own face, personality, greeting and preferred model. It runs on the models on your computer.

Get started

  1. Install Bouncer. Run one line in a terminal, or download an installer from bouncer-ai.pages.dev.
    curl -fsSL https://bouncer-ai.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
    irm https://bouncer-ai.pages.dev/install.ps1 | iex

    The first is for macOS and Linux, the second for Windows PowerShell.

  2. Download a model. The first launch helps you pick one that fits your machine. Any GGUF model from Hugging Face works. See Models in the Bouncer docs.
  3. Open Rebound. Click its icon in the activity bar, or press ⌘ 4 on macOS or Ctrl 4 on Windows and Linux.
  4. Pick a bot and say hi. Click a card to open the bot's room, then start a chat.

The starter bots

Bouncer comes with five bots. You can chat with them as they are, change them, or reset them to how they shipped.

  • Rebound A plain, helpful assistant. Clear answers, no persona.
  • Pip Curious and upbeat. Explains things simply.
  • Juno Witty, a little sarcastic, straight answers.
  • Sage Calm and thoughtful. Good for thinking things through.
  • Quill Your creative writing partner: ideas, drafts, rhymes.

Make a bot

Click New bot at the top of Rebound, or Make a bot in the last tile. The builder has the face on the left and the personality on the right.

Click Create bot to save a new bot, or Save when you edit one. Cancel closes the builder without saving.

Choose a face

Every face is a soft blob drawn from a seed, so a bot keeps its exact shape. Under the preview:

color
eight colors: mint, coral, sky, lemon, grape, peach, rose and slate.
eyes
seven styles: round, happy, sleepy, wink, wide, dots and shades.
shuffle shape
keeps the color and eyes and draws a new shape.
surprise me
picks a whole new random face.

Faces breathe and blink in the app. If your system asks for reduced motion, they hold still. You can also try faces on the home page.

Write a personality

name
required. Keep it short, such as Pip.
tagline
a few words about what the bot is like. It shows on the bot's card.
personality
required. Who the bot is, how it talks and what it cares about. It becomes the bot's system prompt, after "You are <name>."
start from
fills in a starting personality you can edit: friendly, witty, calm, creative, coach or tutor.
traits
up to eight short words, such as curious or patient. Type one and press Enter or ,. Backspace in an empty field removes the last one.
greeting
the first message the bot says in a new chat. Leave it empty for none.

Tips for a personality that holds up

  • Say how it should answer, not only what it is like: "Give the straight answer first, then one light joke."
  • Name what it should avoid: "No long lists unless I ask."
  • Small local models follow short, plain instructions best. A few clear sentences beat a long essay.
  • Read the starters for examples. Open a starter's menu and choose Edit to see its personality.

Creativity and model

creativity
precise gives steady, predictable answers. balanced is the default. creative gives more varied and surprising answers.
model
the model the bot prefers. Whatever is loaded uses the model you have loaded. A bot never switches models on its own.

Chat with a bot

Each bot has a room. The room holds the bot and its chats. Bots and their chats never appear in Bouncer Chat, which is for plain conversations with a model.

  1. Open Rebound and click the bot's card to open its room.
  2. Start a new chat, or continue one listed under the bot.
  3. Type a message and send it. The bot replies in character.

The bot's personality takes the place of chat presets, and it uses its own sampling. To delete a chat, use its trash icon in the room.

Edit, duplicate, reset, delete

Hover a bot's card and open its menu (the three dots):

Edit
opens the builder with the bot's current settings.
Duplicate
makes a copy you can change.
Reset to original
restores a starter bot to how it shipped. Only starter bots have it.
Delete
removes the bot and all of its chats. Duplicate a bot first if you might want it back.

Offline and privacy

Bots run on local models through llama.cpp. Once a model is downloaded, Rebound works with the network off. Bots and their chats are stored on your computer, not in an account, and nothing you type to a bot is sent to a server. See Bouncer's privacy notes for the details.

Questions

Is Rebound a separate app?

No. Rebound is a tab inside Bouncer. Install Bouncer and it is there.

Which model should a bot use?

Any chat model that runs well on your machine. Bigger models follow a personality more closely; smaller ones answer faster. Leave the model on Whatever is loaded to use the one you already have open.

Can a bot see my other chats?

No. A bot only sees the chat you are in with it.

I changed a starter bot. Can I get the original back?

Yes. Open the card's menu and choose Reset to original.

Where do I report a problem?

Open an issue on get-bouncer.
