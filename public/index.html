in the app

Each bot keeps its own chats.

A bot's personality becomes its system prompt. Its chats live with it, apart from Bouncer Chat, and it can use whichever model you pick for it, or the one that is loaded.

on your machine

No account. No server. No cloud bill.

Your bots run on open GGUF models through llama.cpp, on your own computer. Once a model is downloaded, Rebound works with the network off.

Local models

Pick any GGUF model from Hugging Face that fits your machine. Bouncer shows a fit estimate before you download.

Works offline

Chats with your bots never need a connection. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

Yours to keep

Bots and their chats are stored on your computer. Edit them, reset the starters, or delete them whenever you like.

download

Rebound comes with Bouncer.

Install Bouncer in one line, open the Rebound tab, and say hi. The installers pick the newest release and check its SHA256SUMS first.

macOS and Linux

Terminal

curl -fsSL https://bouncer-ai.pages.dev/install.sh | sh

Windows

PowerShell

irm https://bouncer-ai.pages.dev/install.ps1 | iex
